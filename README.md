Overview

The Legacy Trunk (Yaadoo ka Baksa) is a digital family archive that helps preserve memories, heirlooms, and life stories across generations.
It’s a secure storytelling platform where family members can record, upload, and relive shared memories through photos, videos, audio, and text — keeping the heritage alive for years to come.

Problem Statement

Families often struggle to preserve and share their stories, traditions, and emotions in a structured, accessible, and private way.
Our goal is to create a centralized digital archive where families can:

Record and store stories in multiple formats.

Relive life moments through timelines.

Share memories privately within trusted circles.

 Core Idea

“A family’s legacy is its story. We make sure it’s never forgotten.”

The Legacy Trunk acts as a digital family trunk — combining storytelling, memory preservation, and AI assistance to safeguard emotional heritage.

 Process Flow

User Registration & Authentication
Secure sign-up and login using Firebase Authentication.

Family Circle Creation
Admin creates a private family group and invites members.

Story Uploads
Members upload stories in audio, video, image, or text format.

Tagging & Categorization
Smart tagging (manual + AI-assisted) for easy retrieval.

Timeline Generation
Auto-generated family timelines based on story dates.

AI Assistance

Memory prompts to inspire story writing.

AI text analysis for auto-tagging and categorization.

Privacy Control
Share stories only within private family circles.

Export Feature
Download stories as PDFs or create digital family scrapbooks.

 Basic Features

Family Story Recording — Upload and store stories in audio, video, photo, or text form.

Timeline Creation — Build personal and family timelines for life events.

Photo Uploads with Tagging — Add descriptions, tags, and dates for organization.

Private Family Circles — Share memories securely with selected members.

Search Functionality — Find stories, events, or media instantly.

User Roles — Parents, grandparents, and kids can contribute.

Story Export — Convert stories into printable PDFs or books.

 Advanced Features

AI Memory Prompts — Predefined story-starter questions (e.g., “What was your happiest childhood moment?”).

AI Text Analysis — Auto-tagging and content categorization using NLP.

Cross-Generational Matching — Match members with similar interests or experiences.

Collaborative Story Editing — Multiple users can co-edit family stories.

Geo-Tagged Memories — View memories on a world map using Google Maps API.

Time Capsules — Lock stories to be revealed on future dates.

Multilingual Translation — Share stories globally in multiple languages.
