Family Tree Help

Getting Started

  1. Click "Start Your Family Tree" to begin your maternal family tree
  2. Add yourself first, then add your mother, grandmother, etc.
  3. Use the "Add Member" button to add new family members

Adding Family Members

  • Fill out all available information in the form
  • Select the appropriate relationship
  • Choose who they're related to from your existing tree
  • Upload a photo if available

Managing Your Tree

  • Click on any person to view their details
  • Use the Edit button to modify information
  • Expand/Collapse buttons help with larger trees
  • Add children from a person's detail view

Tips & Best Practices

  • Start with older generations and work forward
  • Include birth and death dates when available
  • Add stories and memories in the biography section
  • Update your tree regularly with new information
